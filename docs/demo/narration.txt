Meet Dharma AI, a local book knowledge assistant.

Upload a PDF to begin. The app extracts text, creates overlapping chunks, and generates embeddings with Ollama.

Once indexed, ask a question about the book. Dharma AI retrieves relevant passages and generates an answer grounded in the document.

Here, it correctly identifies the five Pandava brothers. Expand View Sources to inspect the supporting text.

Audio controls let you hear the answer. An unrelated recipe question is rejected, rather than answered from general knowledge.

Voice input and spoken answers were also tested successfully by the project owner.

This recorded walkthrough stays available even when the optional local demo is offline.
